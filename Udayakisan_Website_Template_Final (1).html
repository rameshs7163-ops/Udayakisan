The Challenges Farmers Face

🌱 Crop Diseases

Crop diseases can reduce crop quality and farmer income.

⚠️ Fake Inputs

Farmers may struggle to identify genuine agricultural inputs.

🗣️ Lack of Guidance

Farmers need simple and reliable cultivation guidance in their local language.

🤝 Finding Buyers

Finding reliable buyers and better market opportunities can be difficult.

Our Solution — Udayakisan

Udayakisan is an AI-powered farming platform designed to support farmers from seed to sale. We bring useful farming information, AI-based support, input verification, market information, and buyer connections together in one platform.

What Udayakisan Offers

📷 Crop Disease Detection

Take a photo of your crop and get support in identifying possible diseases.

✅ Genuine Input Verification

Verify agricultural inputs such as seeds and fertilizers.

🗣️ Local-Language Guidance

Get simple cultivation guidance in your local language.

📈 Market Prices

Check useful local market price information before selling.

🤝 Reliable Buyers

Connect farmers with potential buyers and better market opportunities.
